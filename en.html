Who teaches

Caro Durán

Founder · DJ, production and piano

Colombian composer, multi-instrumentalist and producer based in New York, with over 15 years of experience teaching music in English and Spanish.

  • Part-time faculty, Electronic Digital Instrument, Berklee College of Music
  • Ableton Certified Trainer and Roland Certified Trainer
  • Yamaha Ambassador for Latin America
  • Voting member of the Recording Academy (GRAMMYs) and the Latin Recording Academy

Cindy Rincón

Coordinator · Tiple and Latin American music

Tiple player, music educator and researcher of Latin American music. She coordinates the school and supports every student along the way.

  • B.A. in Music Education, Universidad Industrial de Santander (Colombia)
  • Winner of the Cotrafa Andean Music Festival on the tiple
  • Album of Latin American music with Tierra Andina
  • Director of Rincón Carranguero in New York
