Module 1 · The foundations

What you'll learn

A first module designed for complete beginners: by the end you'll understand how music works at the keyboard and you'll be playing your first pieces.

01

Music theory at the piano

Notes, scales and chords explained directly on the keyboard, so theory always turns into sound.

02

Reading sheet music

Learn to read notes and rhythm on the staff, step by step, until reading feels natural.

03

Your first pieces

Take your first steps at the piano and build the basics to play your first complete pieces.

04

A touch of jazz

Discover the sound of jazz from the start, so you learn the foundations with style and groove.
